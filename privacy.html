Tulos Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 20, 2026

Tulos does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Period.

  • No data collection — the app has no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs.
  • No network access — Tulos makes no network connections of any kind. Everything happens on your device.
  • No accounts — there is nothing to sign up for and nothing to log in to.
  • Local storage only — your games (player names, colors, scores) and settings are saved only in the app’s local storage on your device, purely so a game survives between launches and your last group is remembered. They never leave your device, and deleting the app deletes them.
  • Voice announcements — spoken scores use the speech voices built into iOS, entirely offline. Nothing is recorded and nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Live Activities — the lock-screen scoreboard is rendered by iOS on your device from the same local game data.

Because Tulos collects nothing, there is nothing to share, sell, or lose. This policy can only get shorter.
